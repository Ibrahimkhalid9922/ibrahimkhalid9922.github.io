Invoxa: Invoice Maker Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Invoxa: Invoice Maker app ("the app") handles and why. The app is published by Ibrahim Chaudhary ("we").

No account, no servers

The app works without sign-up or login. We do not run any server for the app, and we do not receive or store your business details, clients, products or invoices.

Information you enter

Your business profile, logo, signature, clients, saved items and invoices are stored only on your device. This information leaves your device only when you choose to share, print or save a PDF, or save a backup file to a location you pick (for example Google Drive). Uninstalling the app deletes this data from your device.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • device and app information, and ad interactions (such as views and taps).

This is handled by Google under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (Settings, Google, Ads).

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings, Privacy choices.

Permissions

  • Internet: to load ads.
  • Photos: only when you choose a logo; the image is copied into the app.

Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new date.
